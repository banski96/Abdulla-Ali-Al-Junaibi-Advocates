Protecting Heirs, Wealth & Family Guardianship

A Will is a legal document that identifies the key heirs of the estate and all the wealth that comes down after a person passes away or during the life of the individual. We provide assistance, support and service for drafting of the will, to its attestation from the notary till representation of inheritors and beneficiaries with their petitions in UAE Courts.

The actual Will document covers in terms of movable assets to be items that are part of an individual's day to day use (for e.g. cars, gold, cash, personal belongings, investments etc.) and immovable assets in terms of property.

The will also pertains to managing the rights of the minor who are yet not capable of deciding their future decisions. Through the will the guardianship of the minors can also be discussed and issued (only for Non-Muslims).
